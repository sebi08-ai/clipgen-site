Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Deutsch weiter unten

1. Who we are

ClipGen is a private, locally running tool operated by an individual for their own YouTube and TikTok channels. Contact: KONTAKT_EMAIL

2. Data we access

  • Google / YouTube (only accounts the operator connects): permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the connected channel's name (youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok (only accounts the operator connects): basic profile information such as the display name (user.info.basic) and permission to send videos as drafts to the account's inbox (video.upload).
  • Public video clips and metadata from streaming platforms, used to create the operator's own videos.

3. How we use it

Access tokens are used only to upload videos that the operator has reviewed and approved, and to show which account is connected. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

4. Storage and sharing

Tokens and all other data are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by ClipGen, and they are not sold or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube and TikTok when a video is uploaded.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the account in ClipGen, which deletes them immediately. You can also revoke access at any time in your Google account permissions or in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). To request deletion of any other data, contact us at the address above.

6. Google and YouTube

ClipGen uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. ClipGen's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. TikTok

TikTok's processing is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

8. Children

ClipGen is not directed at children and is used only by its adult operator.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated.
